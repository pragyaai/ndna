# Large Language Models (LLMs) and nDNA

## Overview

Large Language Models represent the most extensively studied class of foundation models through the nDNA framework. LLMs exhibit rich cognitive patterns that can be analyzed across the three core nDNA dimensions.

## LLM-Specific nDNA Characteristics

### Spectral Curvature in LLMs
- **Attention Layers**: Sharp curvature spikes at attention mechanisms
- **Feed-Forward Networks**: Gradual curvature transitions in processing layers
- **Embedding Layers**: High curvature indicating rich semantic encoding

### Thermodynamic Patterns
- **Token Processing**: Variable energy based on semantic complexity
- **Context Length**: Increasing thermodynamic length with longer contexts
- **Reasoning Tasks**: Elevated energy consumption for complex logical operations

### Belief Vector Dynamics
- **Training Corpus Influence**: Strong directional alignment with training data biases
- **Fine-tuning Effects**: Measurable shifts in belief vector orientation
- **Prompt Engineering**: Temporary belief vector deflections
