- **Context expansion**: Gradual increase in spectral curvature as dialogue complexity grows

### Dialogue State Tracking

The neural representation of conversational state evolves through geometric transformations:

$$\mathcal{S}_t = f_{\text{context}}(\mathcal{S}_{t-1}, U_t, H_t)$$

Where:
- $\mathcal{S}_t$: Dialogue state at turn $t$
- $U_t$: User utterance at turn $t$
