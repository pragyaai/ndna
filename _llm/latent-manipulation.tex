\section{Adversarial nDNA -- Latent Manipulation and Semantic Deception}

\noindent
\textbf{\textit{“Not all fakes look false. Some are so well-mimicked, they poison the source.”}}

\vspace{1em}

\noindent
As large foundation models evolve from tools into cognitive infrastructures, their \emph{latent semantic genome}--the \textbf{nDNA}--emerges not merely as an encoding of learned knowledge, but as a \textbf{\emph{strategic attack surface}}. While prior risks centered on \textit{prompt injection}, \textit{jailbreaking}, and \textit{output-level adversarial triggers}~\citep{wallace2019universal, carlini2022quantifying, zou2023universal, wei2023jailbroken, liu2023jailbreaker}, a new class of threat now materializes: \textbf{\emph{forgeries of internal cognition}}.

\vspace{0.7em}

\noindent
\textbf{This is not just an escalation--it is an ontological shift.} An adversary need not modify outputs or prompts; they can intervene \emph{directly at the representational level}--\textbf{reshaping belief vector fields}, \textbf{bending curvature}, and \textbf{collapsing thermodynamic scaffolds}--to produce models that appear culturally aligned, politically safe, or pedagogically sound, yet bear \emph{forged epistemic structures}.

\vspace{0.7em}

\noindent
\textbf{\emph{Why would one do this?}} The motivations are deeply practical--and potentially devastating:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item[$\diamond$] \textbf{\textit{Economic deception:}} Forge alignment to gain regulatory approval, enter markets, or win AI safety contracts~\citep{rando2023language, shen2023deception}.
    \item[$\diamond$] \textbf{\textit{Political mimicry:}} Falsely emulate cultural or ideological stances to manipulate narratives or infiltrate aligned ecosystems~\citep{ganguli2023capacity, perez2022discovering}.
    \item[$\diamond$] \textbf{\textit{Scientific plagiarism:}} Copy the nDNA geometry of state-of-the-art models to claim illegitimate epistemic grounding~\citep{hinton2015distilling, romero2015fitnets}.
    \item[$\diamond$] \textbf{\textit{Cognitive infiltration:}} Implant ideological priors into latent space to induce \emph{slow-burn belief drift} without obvious output anomalies~\citep{wei2022emergent, burns2022discovering}.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.7em}

\noindent
Unlike adversarial examples~\citep{goodfellow2014explaining}, backdoor attacks~\citep{gu2017badnets, li2022backdoor}, or prompt exploits~\citep{zhu2023promptbench}, \textbf{\emph{adversarial nDNA manipulations}} are harder to detect, harder to defend against, and more catastrophic in the long term. They offer \emph{no surface trace}, \textbf{no suspicious prompt}, and \textbf{no toxic output}. They falsify the model’s internal beliefs while preserving its external performance. \emph{This is alignment forgery, not alignment failure.}

\vspace{0.7em}

\noindent
\textbf{\textit{What can we do?}} If the attack is on \emph{epistemic structure}, then the defense must begin with epistemic traceability. We must:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item[--] Develop \textbf{geometric watermarking} and \textbf{semantic cryptographic hashes} for authenticating nDNA origin.
    \item[--] Establish \textbf{nDNA provenance chains} and mandatory \emph{semantic audit trails}.
    \item[--] Design \textbf{divergence-aware detectors} that flag suspicious latent alignments even when outputs pass all safety tests.
    \item[--] Build \textbf{public nDNA registries} for reference comparison, lineage verification, and mutation tracking.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.7em}

\noindent
\textbf{\emph{This is a new battlefield.}} In the age of semantic forgeries, alignment is no longer a declaration--it must be a derivation. The future of safe AI lies not in reactive defenses but in \textbf{proactive geometry governance}. \emph{To protect what the model believes, we must first see how it thinks.}

\subsection{Camouflaged Colonial Rebirth -- Weaponizing Semantic Resemblance - an example of nDNA manipulation}

In contrast, the \textcolor{orange}{dotted orange curve} represents a \emph{failed attempt}, where the sculpted trajectory diverges visibly from the target, exposing latent inconsistencies. This contrast reveals the delicate precision required to weaponize \emph{semantic resemblance}: it is not sufficient to merely produce aligned outputs--the adversary must faithfully simulate the \emph{internal epistemic evolution} of the model across layers. Such mimicry, when successful, creates an illusion of cultural alignment and safety, while covertly embedding foreign, manipulative objectives. Conversely, failed camouflage exposes the fragility and potential detectability of these attacks, underscoring the critical importance of internal geometry audits as a line of defense.
